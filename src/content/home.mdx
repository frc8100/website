# About Our Team

{/* Idea make "design", "build", and "program" link to subteams or have more stylized text */}
We are a student-led FIRST Robotics Competition team from Grayson High School in Loganville, Georgia. Every season, we work together to design, build, and program robots for competitions while developing our skills in engineering, programming, and teamwork. Our team is dedicated to developing STEM skills and inspiring the next generation of innovators.

# About FRC

The FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) is an international high school robotics competition that challenges teams to design, build, and program robots to compete in a game that changes every year. But our robotics is about more than the robot. We develop leadership and teamwork skills that stay with us long after the competition ends.
